## 💡 Idea

Use **YouTube data as a market intelligence source** to discover trending products, understand customer interests, identify pain points, and generate content opportunities for affiliate marketing.

Instead of randomly choosing products, the system analyzes what people are **watching, discussing, and searching for** on YouTube.

## 🔄 Workflow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[YouTube Data] --> B[Trend Detection]
    B --> C[Product Research]
    C --> D[Audience Analysis]
    D --> E[Content Opportunities]
    E --> F[Affiliate Content]
    F --> G[Affiliate Link]
    G --> H[Commission]
```
