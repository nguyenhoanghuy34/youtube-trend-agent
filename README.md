# YouTube Trend Agent

An AI-powered marketing intelligence agent that discovers, analyzes, and summarizes YouTube trends to help marketers identify opportunities and create data-driven content strategies.

## Marketing Capabilities

| Function | What the Agent Does | Marketing Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **Trend Discovery** | Finds videos and topics that are rapidly gaining traction | Detect emerging and trending topics early |
| 🧠 **Trend Summarization** | Summarizes the content of multiple trending videos | Understand trends without watching hundreds of videos |
| 📊 **Trend Analysis** | Analyzes views, likes, comments, engagement, and growth velocity | Evaluate whether a trend is truly worth pursuing |
| 🎯 **Audience Insight** | Analyzes topics, audience reactions, and comments | Understand what customers and audiences care about |
| 💡 **Content Ideas** | Generates content ideas based on current trends | Create videos, posts, and campaigns that leverage trends |
| 📢 **Campaign Intelligence** | Connects emerging trends with products, brands, and campaigns | Identify marketing opportunities and potential campaign angles |

## Overall Workflow

| Stage | Output |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | Trending videos and emerging topics |
| **Summarize** | Key information from trending content |
| **Analyze** | Trend strength, engagement, and growth |
| **Understand** | Audience interests and sentiment |
| **Generate** | Content ideas and campaign concepts |
| **Recommend** | Marketing opportunities and actions |

> **Goal:** Turn YouTube trend data into actionable marketing intelligence.
